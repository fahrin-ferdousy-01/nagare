One night. No fixed shore. ・ 流れ市

Tonight, the market moves with the tide.

Board a lantern skiff at dusk and follow the red gate into seven kitchens, an old-water shrine, and a teahouse that closes only when the current turns.

03

The old-water crossing ・ 橋と社

Before the first cup
cross somewhere older.

The curved bridge leaves the market grid for one quiet island. Gold marks the way in. Red marks the way back.

A small ritual

  1. Pause where the bridge begins.
  2. Ring once for the water below.
  3. Return by the red-leaf side.
04

The lantern signal ・ 灯籠塔

When the tower answers
the whole market glows.

At the turning of the hour, one chamber becomes a beacon. Every stall responds from the gate to the last jetty.

20:12OPEN

The lighting is a ritual, not a switch. Watch it from the bridge before entering the square.

The last warm room ・ 茶屋

Stay for one pot.
Let the market drift.

The upper room faces the tower; the lower veranda faces open water. Both serve until the final skiff unmoors.
